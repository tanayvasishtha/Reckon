# Sample ledger attribution

These files are public records, committed so Reckon runs without a download.

## Los Angeles (`la_sample.csv`)

Checkbook L.A., City of Los Angeles Controller, CC BY 4.0

Source: https://controllerdata.lacity.org/Purchasing/Checkbook-L-A-Data/pggv-e4fn

20,000 rows from fiscal year 2024.

## Oklahoma (`ok_sample.csv`)

State of Oklahoma Vendor Payments, Office of Management and Enterprise Services, CC BY

Source: https://data.ok.gov/dataset/d58f70a8-1885-418b-8d6c-02bb38f5c1c2

5,000 rows from `vendors_public_2021q1.csv`.
